🎬 Convert Video

Convert, shrink or trim videos, or extract their audio. Powered by ffmpeg running on your device.

⚠️ Conversion runs on your device in a single thread, so it is several times slower than a desktop app. It works best for short clips — roughly under 200 MB.

This file is large. Conversion may take a long time or run out of memory — trimming or lowering the resolution helps.

Resolution only scales down. Times can be seconds (75) or minutes:seconds (1:15).
