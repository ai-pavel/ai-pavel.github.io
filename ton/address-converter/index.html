🔄 Address Converter

Convert a TON address between raw and user-friendly forms, and check its checksum.

What the forms mean

Raw is workchain:hash. User-friendly addresses are 36 bytes in base64: a flags byte, the workchain, the 32-byte hash and a CRC16 checksum.

Bounceable (EQ…, testnet kQ…): if the receiving contract fails, the coins bounce back. Use it for smart contracts. Non-bounceable (UQ…, testnet 0Q…): coins stay even if the account isn't deployed yet. Wallets show this form.

The testnet flag only marks the address as meant for testnet; the account hash is the same.
