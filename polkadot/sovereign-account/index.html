🏛️ Sovereign Account

Get a parachain's sovereign accounts: the account it controls on the relay chain and on sibling parachains.

Account id = the ASCII tag (para on the relay chain, sibl on other parachains) followed by the parachain ID as a little-endian u32, padded with zeros to 32 bytes. These are the accounts XCM uses when a parachain acts on another chain.
